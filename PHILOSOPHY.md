# Philosophy

Experience Compiler exists to turn machine-readable business semantics into explicit, reviewable interaction structures.

Principles:
- Enterprise truth belongs upstream; interaction truth does not replace business truth.
- Compilation is deterministic: same pinned input + compiler version => same UIDL.
- Human verification is a first-class capability, not a temporary workaround.
- Output is runtime-neutral. Eidos is a target runtime, not a hidden dependency.
- AI may propose layouts or interaction plans later, but generated interaction remains versioned and inspectable.
- Do not rely on chat memory. Repository contracts and executable tests are authoritative.
