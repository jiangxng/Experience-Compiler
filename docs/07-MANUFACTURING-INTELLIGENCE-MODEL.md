# Manufacturing Intelligence Model

The manufacturing domain is modeled around decisions, not ERP screens.

Core semantic families:
- demand and customer promise
- material availability and supplier reliability
- BOM/routing/work-center constraints
- production capacity, schedule and WIP
- quality, yield, scrap and rework
- inventory, shortage and substitution
- maintenance/downtime
- cost, margin and working capital
- fulfillment/OTIF
- exceptions, decisions and outcomes

Reference decision families:
- material shortage response
- expedite vs substitute vs reschedule
- capacity overload resolution
- quality containment/release
- maintenance now vs defer
- allocation among competing customer orders
- supplier escalation/change
- make/buy and alternate routing

Every future recommendation should preserve evidence, assumptions, business impact, alternatives, authority requirements and observed outcome.
