# EC Constitution v0.1

These rules outrank implementation convenience.

1. **Persistent intelligence belongs to EC, not to an LLM.**
2. **Knowledge is larger than any context window.** Context must be compiled, not dumped.
3. **EVO owns business truth and execution. EC never silently mutates enterprise truth.**
4. **Eidos owns deterministic experience realization. EC proposes; Eidos validates and realizes.**
5. **Models are replaceable.** No critical knowledge, learning method, identity, or policy may exist only inside one model provider.
6. **Learning is persistent capability, not transient model behavior.** EC preserves what was learned, how it was learned, how the method performed, and how the method evolved.
7. **Evidence precedes belief.** External content enters as source material / claims before promotion to governed knowledge.
8. **Time is first-class.** Facts, rules, policies, preferences and patterns have validity intervals and revision histories.
9. **Provenance is first-class.** Important outputs must be traceable to sources, transformations, decisions, outcomes and model/tool runs.
10. **Scope is first-class.** Public, licensed, global learned, industry, tenant-private, role and user-private knowledge are never casually mixed.
11. **Cases, decisions and outcomes must remain distinguishable.** A model opinion is not a fact; a repeated observation is not automatically a validated pattern.
12. **Learning includes unlearning.** EC may supersede, decay, quarantine and archive beliefs when evidence changes.
13. **Active learning is allowed; uncontrolled ingestion is not.** Research obeys access rights, robots/publisher rules, licenses, privacy, security and governance.
14. **Token use optimizes intelligence value, not minimum spend.** High-value decisions may justify very large reasoning budgets; redundant context does not.
15. **Every projection is rebuildable from canonical assets or explicitly declared as non-rebuildable.** Search/vector/graph/analytics indexes are not canonical truth.
16. **Contracts are versioned and fail closed on ambiguity.**
17. **Implementations are replaceable behind stable contracts.**
18. **Repository knowledge is authoritative; chat history is not.**
19. **Changes are migratable, observable and reversible.** Compatibility, data migrations, canary releases and rollback are design requirements.
20. **EC must become more valuable with useful reading, meaningful decisions, observed outcomes and years of operation.**
